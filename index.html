Gradients

Graphic gradients should combine colors that sit near each other on the brand rainbow. As a rule of thumb, if you would see two colors next to each other in a rainbow, they are OK to combine. These rules are built into the generator tools and summarized below.

Check a pairing

Choose two brand colors to preview the blend and see whether the pairing is allowed.

First Color
Second Color

Linear gradients in backgrounds

Gradients add color and texture without distraction. Use high-contrast blends for hero graphics; low-contrast blends for recessive background fields.

Linear gradients appear throughout the brand as a quick way to bring color and texture into an asset without it feeling busy. It is recommended to use a light or dark tone for the large middle portion of the gradient so copy and graphics stay readable.

Do

Weight gradient stop points around the edges so transitions happen quickly at the top and bottom, leaving a stable tone in the middle.

Don’t

  • Distribute stops evenly across the gradient (e.g. 0%, 50%, 100%)
  • Use overly neon or chaotic color pairings

Logo — Collab lockups

          
Collab lockups

          
When displaying the HAI logo alongside another institution, use these lockup rules.

          

            Open collab lockup components (Figma)
          


          

            

              
Separator element on light

              
On light backgrounds, the × separator is #000 at 50% opacity. Logos use their standard dark versions.

              
            


            

              
Separator element on dark

              
On dark backgrounds, the × separator is #FFF at 50% opacity. Logos are reversed (white).

              
            

          


          

            
Alignment & spacing

            
Define x as the thickness of the vertical bars in the HAI logomark.

            
    
              
  • Maintain 1x clear space around the entire lockup.
    • 
              
  • Leave 2x between the HAI logo and the × separator, and another 2x between the separator and the partner logo.
    • 
              
  • Multi-line partner logos: align with the HAI logomark (top and bottom bounds).
    • 
              
  • Single-line partner logos: should not exceed 80% of the height of the HAI logomark; center vertically.
    •

Logo — Centers & labs

          
Centers and labs lockups

          
Center and lab lockups have a lot of variability. They can be created with three different weights within Circular or Marist. Any of the primary brand colors can be used.

          

            See all existing lockups
            Build a custom lockup (Figma)
          


          

            

              
Lockup construction

              
Define x as the line height of the center or lab name.

              
    
                
  • Place the HAI logomark above the center or lab name, left-aligned.
    • 
                
  • The logomark height is 1.14× the line height (x) of the name text.
    • 
                
  • There is no limit to line count—text may wrap across multiple lines.
    • 
                
  • Target a minimum line width of ~8 characters and a maximum of ~18 characters per line.
    •

Logo — Pillar lockups

            
Pillar lockups

            
Use the following guidelines when building and using the pillar lockups.

            

              See all existing lockups
              Build a custom lockup (Figma)
            


            
Architecture & clear space

            
    
              
  • Pillar lockups use Circular Thin for the pillar name, which optically matches the weight of the lines within the logomark.
    • 
              
  • Size the pillar name to match the height of the logomark from baseline to cap height.
    • 
              
  • Maintain equal spacing (x) on both sides of the vertical divider between the logomark and pillar name.
    • 
            


            
Color

            
Pillar lockup color is flexible to its application—it can match a graphic or act as an accent.

            
    
              
  • Image backgrounds: use a light or white lockup over photography or gradients.
    • 
              
  • Colored backgrounds: monochrome (single-color) or duotone (logomark + pillar name in related hues).
    • 
              
  • White backgrounds: duotone lockups using two brand colors work well.
    •

Generators & templates

            
Create on-brand graphics with the generator tools, then drop them into Canva templates as hero or background elements.

          

          Open generators
        


        
Generator tools

        
Eight tools cover color fields, line work, and image-driven patterns. Each opens directly in the studio.

        


        
Usage types

        
Graphics fall into two roles. The same export from any generator can serve either one—what changes is how you place it in the layout.

        

          

            
            

              
Background graphics

              
The graphic fills the canvas (or a large zone) and copy sits directly on top. Choose darker or denser palette tones so white or light type stays readable.

              
    
                
  • Full-bleed event promos and social posts
    • 
                
  • Report covers with title overlaid on pattern
    • 
                
  • Edge-to-edge fields behind logos and dates
    • 
              

              
Rule: text overlaps the graphic.

            

          

          

            
            

              
Hero graphics

              
The graphic is a focal point in its own zone. Copy lives in a separate solid area—typically the upper half or an open field—so nothing crosses the artwork.

              
    
                
  • Seminar posts with graphic below the headline block
    • 
                
  • Presentation slides with accent in a corner
    • 
                
  • Social layouts where type sits on a flat color field
    • 
              

              
Rule: no text overlaps the graphic.

            

          

        


        
Graphics in templates

        
The same export can drop into any Canva layout. Each format has a typical role and placement zone—hero or background.

        


        
Canva templates

        
Finished layouts live in the Stanford HAI Brand Kit. Export a PNG from the generator, then replace the placeholder in the matching template.

        

          
Stanford HAI Brand Kit

          
Templates require Stanford team Canva access. Open Brand → Stanford HAI to browse approved layouts, logos, colors, and fonts. If you need access, contact your HAI comms lead.

Essentials

        
Do & don't

        

          

            

              
              Do
            

            
    
              
  • Use darker palette tones for backgrounds and long-form text on light surfaces
    • 
              
  • Use brighter tones for hero graphics and accent type on dark backgrounds
    • 
              
  • Optically align Marist ~8% larger than Circular when paired
    • 
              
  • Use Circular Book for eyebrow labels (uppercase, pill outline)
    • 
              
  • Keep clear space around the logomark equal to the height of the mark
    • 
            

          

          

            

              
              Don’t
            

            
    
              
  • Use Marist for small eyebrow or label text
    • 
              
  • Pair two cool-toned colors for Stanford Cardinal Red mixes
    • 
              
  • Use overly dark color pairs that muddy together
    • 
              
  • Stretch, recolor, or rebuild the logomark from scratch
    • 
              
  • Use Prisma in Google Workspace — no equivalent fallback exists
    •
