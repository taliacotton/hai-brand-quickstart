Brand at a glance

Distinct yet Stanford

HAI must feel distinctive while making its place within Stanford clear — connected to the university, not interchangeable with it.

Human-centered

Warm, editorial typography paired with clean geometric sans-serifs. Color and graphics should feel vibrant, not corporate.

When in doubt

Use darker tones for backgrounds and body text on light surfaces. Use brighter tones for graphics and accents on dark backgrounds.

Logo system

Primary logomark, horizontal & vertical lockups, centers & labs lockups, pillar lockups, and collab lockups.

Essentials

Do & don't

Do

  • Use darker palette tones for backgrounds and long-form text on light surfaces
  • Use brighter tones for hero graphics and accent type on dark backgrounds
  • Optically align Marist ~8% larger than Circular when paired
  • Use Circular Book for eyebrow labels (uppercase, pill outline)
  • Keep clear space around the logomark equal to the height of the mark

Don't

  • Use Marist for small eyebrow or label text
  • Pair two cool-toned colors for Stanford Red mixes
  • Use overly dark color pairs that muddy together
  • Stretch, recolor, or rebuild the logomark from scratch
  • Use Prisma in Google Workspace — no equivalent fallback exists
